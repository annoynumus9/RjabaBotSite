Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Rjava Bot ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

By using the Bot or its associated services, including Roblox account verification, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The Bot may collect and store information including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Discord Role IDs
  • Discord Message IDs (when required)
  • Roblox User IDs
  • Roblox usernames
  • Verification status
  • Server configuration
  • Moderation settings
  • Audit logs and moderation records
  • Command usage required to operate the Bot

We do not intentionally collect passwords or authentication credentials.

3. Roblox Integration

If you link your Roblox account, the Bot may store the association between your Discord account and Roblox account in order to provide verification and synchronization features.

Public Roblox profile information may be accessed as needed to perform requested actions.

4. How Information Is Used

Your information may be used to:

  • Provide moderation features.
  • Verify Roblox account ownership.
  • Synchronize Discord roles with Roblox groups.
  • Store server settings.
  • Maintain moderation logs.
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized use.
  • Diagnose bugs and improve reliability.

5. Data Sharing

Information collected by the Bot is not sold to third parties.

Information may only be shared when required by law, necessary to investigate abuse, or with service providers that assist in operating the Bot.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Server administrators may remove the Bot at any time, after which associated server data may be deleted according to our retention practices.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect stored information. However, no system can guarantee complete security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account.

Requests may be submitted through the Bot's official support server.

9. Third-Party Services

The Bot interacts with third-party services including Discord and Roblox. Your use of those services is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

10. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from individuals except as necessary to provide its services through Discord and Roblox.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the Bot after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the Bot's official support server or other published support channels.
